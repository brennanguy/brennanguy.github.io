Why these fonts

IBM Plex Mono for headings
Its even, machine-written asthetic suggests coded transmissions and classified terminal records, fitting Eleutheria’s work in the shadows, and serve to remind Shadow of the early days when he manned the site himself. The h1 is 3rem to give the organization name authority; h2 is 2rem for clear report sections; h3 is 1.375rem for alert labels that remain subordinate.
System sans-serif for reading
Familiar letter shapes keep long field reports readable when they must be scanned quickly. Too many people complained about the IBM's readibility, and for field work fancy fonts can't be taken for granted. Body text is 1.125rem, and the sample quote is 1.25rem to set it apart without competing with a heading.
Line height
Body copy uses a unitless line-height of 1.6, leaving enough space between lines for tired night-shift readers to track each report.
