About this theme

This visual system belongs to our secret organization dedicated to defending the world from the shadows against the return of the Ordo. Abyss and Steel blue give our identity a quiet, controlled foundation. Cipher keeps reports readable, while Relic gold marks important actions and signals. The result should feel disciplined and watchful, not flashy.
