About the show

Merlin (also known as The Adventures of Merlin) was a live-action BBC television show that ran from 2008 to 2012. This retelling of Arthurian legend follows a young Merlin as he seeks to help Camelot while keeping his magical abilities a secret. The show combines humor and drama in its reinterpretation of the legend.
