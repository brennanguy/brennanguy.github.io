About

Merlin (also known as The Adevntures of Merlin) was a live-action BBC television show that ran from 2008-2012. This retelling of Arthurian legend follows a young Merlin as he seeks to help Camelot while keeping his magical abilities a secret. I really enjoy it's creative reinterpretation of the legend and the seemless blend of humor and drama it brings to the table.
