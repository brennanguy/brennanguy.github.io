File 014

The Eye Behind the Crown

A group known as "The Crimson Crown" were apprehended after failing to commandeer a passenger airplane. Despite their claims to work alone, information from a local policeman tells us that, somehow, the pilot and copilot were killed by this group while the passengers had a clear view to the terrorists in the back of the plane. Even stranger, this policeman has told us that the victims fatal wounds were apparently severe, 3rd degree burns, and the plane was only reported to have entered its nosedive after the passengers successfully apprehended the terrorists. Combine that with the fact that no one saw the trio of criminals board the plane, and mysterious scorch-marks found in the bathroom, and this seems like it may be the work of the mystical Illuminati I've warned you all of.

File 027

The Truth Behind "The Snake"

As we all know, rural Oklahoma was plagued by a mysterious string of odd murders, the earliest starting five years ago. The bodies recovered had died of snake toxin, and each was found with a pair of fang-like puncture wounds somewhere on their bodies. Sparce eyewitness accounts in the area also reported strange hissing noises and glimpses of a truly massive serpentine form in the shadows, which are too coincidental for even the authrities to ignore. With more strange happenings being reported across the country, it seems something mysterious has been happening. Strangely enough, about a year ago, the very last sighting of this serpent was reported, following the disapearance of a boy name Janus Ree. Ever since his disappearance, the murders have stopped too. Could Janus have been this "Snake"? After all, nearly all victims of "The Snake" had some close friendship to Janus; could he have been leading these victims to their deaths? Not to mention that his last girlfriend prior to his disappearance, the infamous Amy Weatherton, murdered her new boyfriend less than a week later under mysterious circumstances. And Amy's family died in a mysterious car crash a few days before that, except for her sister, the only survivor, who is now missing. Could this all be related to "The Snake"? Or is " The Snake" really gone for good? The truth is out there, and we will find it.
